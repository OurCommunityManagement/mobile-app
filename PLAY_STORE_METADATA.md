## Full description

Community App helps residents and community teams stay connected through one simple and secure mobile experience.

With Community App, users can:

- view dues and invoices
- pay community charges
- access payment history and receipts
- manage profile details
- register and track visitors
- book amenities
- review community information in one place

Community administrators can also manage operational workflows through the same connected platform, helping communities stay organized and responsive.

Built for real community operations, the app is connected to the same secure backend used by the web portal so data stays synchronized across devices.
